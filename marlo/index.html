Why our books are sewn, not glued.

A glued spine cracks the first time you press a book flat on a rock to sketch. A sewn one outlives you. It costs us £1.40 more a copy.

05 — Questions

Buying, gifting, submitting.

Do you ship outside the UK?

Yes, to Europe and North America. Postage is £4 in the UK, £9 to Europe, and £14 elsewhere, free on orders over £60.

What is in the season box?

All four new books of the season, posted together in March and October, for £58 instead of £70. Cancel any time before the next box ships.

Can I buy a box as a gift?

Yes. Choose one season or a full year, and we will include a handwritten card with your message.

Do you accept unsolicited manuscripts?

Yes, every January. Send three sample chapters and a page on where the book takes place. We read everything and reply within 10 weeks.

Where can I find Marlo books in shops?

In 64 independent bookshops across Britain, most visitor centres in the Cairngorms, and at our office shop in Leith on Saturdays.
