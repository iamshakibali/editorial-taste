04 — Questions

Good to know before you come.

Is there a minimum stay?

Two nights from April to October, three in July and August. One night is fine the rest of the year.

Can we bring children?

Yes, in the cottage and the courtyard rooms. The terrace rooms sit on the cliff edge, so they are for guests over 12.

Do we need a car?

It helps. Without one, we collect you from the Odemira bus stop for €25, and the village is a 15 minute walk.

Are dogs allowed?

One well behaved dog is welcome in the cottage for €20 a night. The beach paths are dog friendly outside July and August.

Is the water warm enough to swim?

The Atlantic here is 17 to 21 °C in summer. The river beach is calmer and a few degrees warmer, and the pool is always the easy option.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival. After that, we keep the first night.
